# en-dark-romance 正向样例

以下样例是为这个风格新写的，学的是语气，不要照抄。

## 开场

> The man who bought my father’s debt did it on a Tuesday, in cash, and sent me the receipt.
>
> No note. Just the amount and a time: nine o’clock, the back room at Moretti’s. I’m an accountant. I read the receipt twice, found the error in his favor, and brought a corrected copy.

## 危险靠行为

> “Your landlord called,” he said. “He’s decided you don’t owe him the back rent after all.”
>
> “I didn’t ask you to do that.”
>
> “No.” He turned his glass a quarter turn on the bar. “He has a nice family. He told me all their names.”

## 女主的筹码

> “You’ve got three sets of books,” I said. “The third one is wrong. I found it in an hour. If I can, the Feds can.”
>
> He didn’t move. Behind him, the man at the door stopped pretending to read his phone.
>
> “So here’s my rate,” I said.

## 章尾钩子

`reveal`，强度 4：

> I found the second phone taped under his desk drawer. One contact. One message, sent the night my father disappeared.
>
> *It’s done. The daughter doesn’t know.*

`decision`，强度 4：

> “Two tickets,” he said, and laid them on the table. “The car leaves at midnight. You can be on it alone, or you can tell me who you’ve been talking to.”
