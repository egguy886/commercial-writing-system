> Nina Park had been a veterinarian for six years and had been bitten by exactly one patient, which was why she took it personally when the man in Exam Room 2 said his corgi was “a great judge of character.”
>
> The corgi had his teeth in her sleeve.
>
> “He likes you,” the man said.
