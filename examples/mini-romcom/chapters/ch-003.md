# Chapter 3: Line Four

Theo stood in the door with two coffees, as if it were a normal hour for a CFO to be anywhere.

“You’re still here,” he said.

“You put two spaces after a period.”

He set one coffee on her desk and stayed on his side of it. “Is that a yes to the coffee, or an accusation?”

“Both.” Her phone buzzed. It was her mother, the only person alive who still called her Danielle, and Dana let it ring. She stood up. He didn’t move, so she came around the desk herself and took him by the tie.

“Say it,” she said.

Behind him, the door swung open. Priya stopped with her hand on the frame and her badge in her teeth.
