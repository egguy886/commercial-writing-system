# Chapter 1: The Apology Invoice

Dana Cho found the error at 9:14, which was late for her. The Marlowe Bakery invoice listed forty croissants and one apology, and the apology had been billed at nine hundred dollars.

“You can’t expense an apology,” she said.

Theo Marsh leaned on the wall of her cubicle. “Watch me.”

“It’s line four.”

“It was a good apology.”

She flagged it in red and went to put the file away, next to the brass key nobody had ever asked her about. When she sat back down, her phone had lit up.

*Stop auditing the bakery. I know what’s in your drawer.*
