# Chapter 2: Punctuation

She pulled the drawer all the way out and set it on her knees. One stapler. One brass key. Eleven granola bars she would deny under oath.

Whoever had sent the text had put two spaces after the period. Dana knew exactly one person under fifty who did that. He had once sent her a two-space memo about the Marlowe Bakery account.

It proved nothing. She wrote it down anyway, and stayed until the cleaners had gone and the floor went dark.

At 11:40, somebody knocked on the glass.
