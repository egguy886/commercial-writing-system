# Chapter 4: Disclosure

Priya took the badge out of her mouth. “It’s seven in the morning,” she said. “Why does it smell like a bad decision in here?”

Dana let go of the tie.

“It’s my fault,” Theo said, and turned so that the three people now gathered at the door could hear him. “I sent the text. The bakery belongs to my sister, and I’ve been paying its debts through this firm. Ms. Cho found it in a week.”

Nobody breathed.

Dana opened her hand and held out the brass key. “Then you’ll want this. It opens the records room, and I already copied everything.”

He took it. “Line four,” he said.

“Still not deductible.”
